# WSR88 · Write Share Read

A minimal, elegant, and interactive social micro-blogging platform.  
Share your thoughts, express your ideas, and connect with a community that values creativity and expression.

**Powered by Google Apps Script.** No databases to manage. No complex hosting. Just pure lightweight interaction.

---

### ✨ Why use this?
- A judgment-free space to write, share, and read short notes.
- A visual platform where every post can be voted on using **Red, Green, or Blue** dots to express feelings or agreement.
- Designed to be simple, fast, and mobile-friendly.

---

### 🔧 Key Features
- **👤 Simple Account System:** Create a unique ID and name (max 10 chars, 3-digit ID) to start sharing.
- **📝 Rich Post Creation:** Write text and upload images directly from your device.
- **🎨 3-Color Voting System:** React to posts using Red, Green, or Blue dots. Your vote is remembered locally.
- **🌓 Dark/Light Theme:** Toggle between a relaxing dark mode and a clean light mode.
- **📅 Archive Filter:** Filter posts by date to revisit your favorite moments.
- **💾 Local Storage:** Your session and votes are saved locally for a seamless experience.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Works perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.

---

### 🚀 Quick Start
1. Open the platform.
2. Click **"Sign Up"** to create your profile (choose a name and a 3-digit ID like `088`).
3. Write your thought in the main text area and optionally add an image.
4. Click **"Publish"** to share it with the community.
5. Browse the feed and vote on others' posts using the colored dots.
